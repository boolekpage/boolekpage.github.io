SOLGROFF

SOLGROFF is software designed for the calculation and design of off-grid photovoltaic systems, available at a highly affordable price. It is based on a grid-agnostic approach, allowing for simple, fast, and effective preliminary designs for installations independent of the electrical grid. It features a one-time payment model, does not require an internet connection to operate, and is compatible with Windows 7, 8.1, 10, and 11 operating systems.
